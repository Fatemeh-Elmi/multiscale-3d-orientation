Important interpretation
------------------------
P86 is used only to define image-derived candidate regions in experimental data.
It is not used for synthetic phantom evaluation, which uses geometry-derived
ground-truth masks. The P86 mask is not a chemically specific collagen
segmentation. Experimental data do not provide independent voxel-wise 3-D
orientation ground truth, so the experimental analysis is a transfer/reliability
demonstration rather than an absolute-accuracy validation.
